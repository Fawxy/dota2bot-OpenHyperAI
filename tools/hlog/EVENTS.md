# Humaniser events (generated)

Generated by `node tools/hlog/gen_events.js` from `typescript/bots/FunLib/humaniser/events.ts`; do not edit. Every
`[HEV] ev=<name>` line the humaniser prints through `Telemetry.Event` is listed with its fields. `bool` prints as
1/0. Any field may be absent on a line (a nil value is not printed). The store reads the same declaration from
`events.json` (`str` columns are text; the others are inferred from the values).

125 typed events.

## lua_error

A Lua error caught by a Guard, or one escaping an OHA entry point (`where=oha.<file>.<fn>`, `Telemetry.Entry`, which re-raises it unchanged). Repeats update `n`.

`pid` mixed, `tb` str

- `where` str: The guard site, or `oha.<file>.<fn>` for an error escaping an OHA entry point (mode `GetDesire`/`Think`/`OnStart`/`OnEnd`, item and ability thinks).
- `n` num: Count so far; `check` counts the highest `n` once per where and msg, not the sum of the lines.
- `msg` str: The error message; `"` is written as `'`, so free text is lossy.

## front

Once a second per team: the team's wave front in each lane, from `GetLaneFrontLocation`. Read by `@front_at`.

- `team` num: The writing team. Amounts follow its lane path, so the two teams' amounts for one place differ; compare within one team's lines, or across teams by map position.
- `top` num: Top lane front, as an amount along the lane: 0 at Radiant's ancient to 1 at Dire's.
- `topx` num: Top front, map x.
- `topy` num: Top front, map y.
- `mid` num: Mid lane front amount (as `top`).
- `midx` num: Mid front, map x.
- `midy` num: Mid front, map y.
- `bot` num: Bot lane front amount (as `top`).
- `botx` num: Bot front, map x.
- `boty` num: Bot front, map y.

## bot

One line per bot at start and again when its profile or position changes. The only event that carries `schema`, `build` and `ours`.

`pid` num, `hero` str, `team` num, `humaniser` bool, `skill` num, `ally` bool, `notice` num, `react` num, `ping` num, `chat` num, `jitter` num, `lapse` num, `lapseMax` num, `minReact` num, `deference` num

- `schema` num: Log format version.
- `build` str: The bot build as `<sha>[+dirty]`; always a string, even when all digits.
- `pos` num: The lineup position, 1-5.
- `ours` str: Every `Ours` switch in name order as `Switch:1|0,...`. Views call a hero ours when a switch is on, else OHA (`@rule_counts`).

## creep_death

An ENEMY lane creep (as seen by the logging team) that was seen last frame and is gone this frame at 50% health or less, within 1,500 of a living own hero. Logged per team by a frame job (`Telemetry.Creeps`, 0:00 to `Telemetry.CreepDeathUntil`, 720 s), in both arms. Mirror: `own_creep_death`.

- `team` num: The logging team: the team whose module saw the death. A row is an enemy lane creep seen by that team, so the same creep may also be in the other team's `own_creep_death`.
- `kind` str: `melee`, `ranged` or `siege`.
- `x` num: The creep's x as last frame read it (a dead creep's handle is never called).
- `y` num: The creep's y as last frame read it.
- `hp` num: The creep's HP as last frame read it.
- `by` mixed: Who got it: the pid of an own hero whose last hits rose this frame (each rise takes its nearest unclaimed death within 1,200), `deny` for a visible enemy hero whose denies rose, else `none`. `deny` has never been seen; view `creep_fate` names enemy denies from the other team's log.
- `tw` bool: 1 when one of the logging team's towers was attacking the creep on the frame before it died.
- `bm` str: The killer's mode name when its last tracked attack on this creep started. Absent when that attack was not tracked (at 8x about a third of right-clicks).
- `bs` num: Seconds from that attack's start to the death; absent with `bm`.
- `p1d` num: Own core at OHA position 1: its distance to the creep. Cores are logged only within 1,500.
- `p1r` num: Position 1 core's attack range.
- `p1t` bool: 1 if the position 1 core was attacking this creep last frame.
- `p1a` num: Seconds since the position 1 core's last attack started, if that attack was on this creep (`GameTime` clock, as `GetLastAttackTime`).
- `p2d` num: Own core at OHA position 2: its distance to the creep (within 1,500 only).
- `p2r` num: Position 2 core's attack range.
- `p2t` bool: 1 if the position 2 core was attacking this creep last frame.
- `p2a` num: Seconds since the position 2 core's last attack started, if on this creep.
- `p3d` num: Own core at OHA position 3: its distance to the creep (within 1,500 only).
- `p3r` num: Position 3 core's attack range.
- `p3t` bool: 1 if the position 3 core was attacking this creep last frame.
- `p3a` num: Seconds since the position 3 core's last attack started, if on this creep.
- `cid` num: The creep's id for this log (Telemetry CreepId; joins an ev=idle_swing).

## lane_read

Slice lane-read, once a second per team and lane to 12:00, both arms. Amounts run from Radiant's ancient (0) but along the writing team's lane path, so they are comparable within one team's lines only (lr1: Dire's read 0.07-0.16 lower). adv 0..2 and a/e/ap/ep over creeps near our front (enemy ones visible only); of/ef fronts, ot/et nearest alive towers; cyc second in the 30 s cycle; cn/chp/cpw/cl own creeps in the whole lane (count, HP, HP-weighted power, lead amount, lead map position clx/cly): the other team's ground truth; cnn/cnp own creeps near the enemy's front (count, power), the enemy reading's e/ep without fog; fx/fy our front's map position. ee per enemy `pid:v|seen:dist[:hp:lvl]` (v = visible, else seconds since seen; hp percent), dead enemies; ag per allied hero `pid:now:since` (since -1 if never). cc every own creep in the lane by ascending amount `kind:amount_in_thousandths:hp` (kind m/r/s/f), tw/twh our nearest alive tower's tier (0 when all are down) and HP (slice lane-census, for the lane forecast's start state and tower crash timing). Slice lane-read-ec: ec every visible enemy lane creep in the lane, as cc; ed per enemy in ee's order `pid:dist_to_our_tower:ally=dist,...` (-1 with no tower); ns alive enemy pids never seen, joined by `;`.

`team` num, `lane` num

- `adv` num: Creep advantage 0..2 (1 even) over the creeps within 1,200 of the team's front, enemy ones visible only.
- `eq` num: Where the waves meet between the nearest towers: -1 at our tower, 1 at theirs.
- `a` num: Own creeps near our front (count).
- `e` num: Enemy creeps near our front (count, visible ones only).
- `ap` num: Own creeps near our front, HP-weighted power.
- `ep` num: Enemy creeps near our front, HP-weighted power (visible ones only).
- `of` num: Our front's amount along the lane.
- `ef` num: Their front's amount along the lane.
- `ot` num: Our nearest alive tower's amount.
- `et` num: Their nearest alive tower's amount.
- `cyc` num: The second in the 30 s wave cycle.
- `cn` num: Own creeps in the whole lane (count): the other team's ground truth, not a reading.
- `chp` num: HP of own creeps in the whole lane.
- `cpw` num: HP-weighted power of own creeps in the whole lane.
- `cl` num: The lead own creep's amount along the lane.
- `clx` num: The lead own creep's map x.
- `cly` num: The lead own creep's map y.
- `cnn` num: Own creeps near the enemy's front (count).
- `cnp` num: Own creeps near the enemy's front (power).
- `fx` num: Our front's map x.
- `fy` num: Our front's map y.
- `dead` num: Enemies dead; also counts enemies found dead before they were seen.
- `ee` str: Per enemy, `pid:v:dist:hp%:lvl` when visible, else `pid:secs_since_seen:dist` from its last-seen spot.
- `ag` str: Per allied hero in the lane, `pid:creeps_on_it_now:secs_since_last` (one decimal, -1 never).
- `cc` str: Every own creep in the lane as `kind:amount_in_thousandths:hp` (kind m, r, s or f), by ascending amount.
- `tw` num: Tier of our nearest alive tower on the lane; 0 when all are down.
- `twh` num: That tower's HP.
- `ec` str: Every visible enemy lane creep in the whole lane, in `cc`'s format.
- `ed` str: Per enemy in `ee`'s order, `pid:dist_to_our_tower:ally=dist,...`; -1 when all our towers on the lane are down.
- `ns` str: Alive enemy pids never seen this game, joined by `;` (in practice only the first 30 s).

## aggro

Slice lane-aggro: one line per draw (lane_aggro).

`apt` num, `lbl` str, `gap` num

- `pid` num: The laner that drew.
- `role` str: `core`, or `support` (its lane core away).
- `kind` str: `feed` (enemy wave onto our ranged creep), `tower` (their creeps drawn out of their tower) or `back` (wave_hold's drag: their wave behind our T1 to our next wave).
- `result` str: feed: `denied` (our deny), `lost` (it died otherwise), `alive` (not dead in FeedWatch); tower: `ok` (a drawn creep out of the tower's reach), `stayed`; back: `ok` (a drawn creep within BackOut of the point behind our T1 at the walk's end), `stayed`; all: `no_draw` (no creep took us within DrawCheck), `hurt`, `died`, `short` (feed: no enemy creep within DrawR at the order after the approach, no order), `cancelled` (before the order: no visible enemy hero to order on, or the feed's ranged creep or the tower move's creep died during the approach), `unsafe` (feed, tower: an enemy hero came into reach of the approach point, or the feed's walk-back point, during the approach), `stale` (not stepped for StaleActing/StaleWatch: the caller lost the frame).
- `tgt` num: The enemy hero the draw order was given on.
- `near` num: Enemy lane creeps within 500 at the order; most targeting the bot during the walk; most on the fed creep after it.
- `drawn` num: Most enemy creeps targeting the hero during the walk.
- `mv` num: Enemy creeps near at the order that moved MovedR since (drawn off their fight), most at once.
- `onr` num: Most enemy creeps on the fed creep after the drop.

- `role` str: The spot's role input: `core` or `support`.
- `who` str: `gate`: the consumer that asked (ev=threat `who`).
- `fm` str: `gate`: the walk's start, x,y (Threat.Gate's `from`).
- `pl` str: `gate`: the walk's end, x,y (the camp or place the trip goes to).
- `lane` num: The bot's assigned lane.
- `b` str: The bot: x, y, HP, max HP, attack range, hull, movement speed, 1 when an escape item is ready.
- `fr` str: GetLaneFrontLocation(team, lane, 0) and (-400): x, y, x, y.
- `fed` num: The spot's `fed` input: lane_aggro's fed creep (CreepId).
- `hold` str: The spot's `hold` input: wave_hold's hold point, x,y.
- `he` str: Enemy heroes in sight within 3,000 of the bot or its lane front (`gate`: of the walk's box): pid, x, y, HP, range, hull, speed, attacking the bot, damaged the bot within Threat EngagedRecent, GetEstimatedDamageToTarget over 3 s and 6 s, illusion.
- `hu` str: Enemy heroes alive and unseen with a sighting: pid, last-seen x, y, seconds unseen, last speed Threat read (-1 none).
- `ha` str: Our other heroes within 3,000 of either: pid, x, y, range, hull, speed, in our retreat.
- `ta` str: Towers standing, hex masks over cut.ts TOWER_IDS: ours, theirs.
- `tt` str: Towers within 3,000 of either: team, TOWER_IDS index, attack target.
- `vis` str: With an unseen enemy: IsLocationVisible on a grid over the bot and its lane front, `x0,y0,nx,ny,step,hex` (cut.ts visionGrid).
- `dec` str: kind=leave: what the game decided, `lethal`, `tower`, `heal`, `threat` (leave) or `under`, `even`, `hold` (stay). Logged when the answer changes, and while an enemy hero is in the read's reach every Retreat CutEvery s (on a frame that made a fresh read).
- `bud` num: kind=leave: the budget the read was held against (by purpose `purp`: free, busy or committed).
- `purp` str: kind=leave: the budget's purpose.
- `ov` num: kind=leave: seconds the read had been over budget before this decision (-1 not over); the hold timer.
- `th` num: kind=leave: Profile.ThreatHold, the seconds the read must stay over budget.
- `unp` num: kind=leave: RetreatOdds' engaged heroes with none of our lane creeps within creep aggro of them.
- `dt` num: kind=leave: 1 when an enemy tower damaged the bot within 0.5 s.
- `thr` num: kind=leave: the read's threat and band, for reading a line without replaying it.
- `vw` str: `gate`, with an unseen enemy: IsLocationVisible at Threat's walk points, `n,hex` (n steps, bit i of the hex the i-th of n + 1 points; cut.ts walkVision).
- `al` num: kind=join (pair_join.ts, D10 in shadow): the ally hit by a hero within PairJoin HitRecent, within AllyRange of the bot (the lowest HP share). Logged every PairJoin Every s while the answer changes, else every CutEvery s, to 12:00. The answer is `dec`: `join`, `outnumbered`, `losing`.
- `po` str: kind=join: our heroes within OursRange of the ally, the bot and the ally included: pid, x, y, HP, max HP, in our retreat.
- `pt` str: kind=join: enemy heroes in sight within FoeRange of the ally: pid, x, y, HP, max HP.
- `dm` str: kind=join: GetEstimatedDamageToTarget over PairJoin Window between the two sides, both ways: from pid, to pid, damage.
- `tgt` num: kind=join: our best target (the enemy our heroes do best on, by damage over its HP), -1 none.
- `ko` num: kind=join: our best kill share (damage over the target's HP) and theirs on ours.

## cut_creeps

The lane creeps of an ev=cut, the same frame and pid (cut.ts).

`pid` num

- `kind` str: `leave` for a kind=leave cut's creeps (absent for the spot's), so two cuts in one frame do not share a line.
- `ce` str: Enemy lane creeps in sight within 3,000 of the bot or its lane front: id, x, y, HP, max HP, ranged, attack target, hull.
- `ca` str: Our lane creeps within 3,000 of either, as `ce`.

## cut_towers

Once per team per game, before its first ev=cut: every standing tower as TOWER_IDS index, x, y, attack range, hull.

`team` num, `tw` str
